# Data protection notes

- Claims contain the claimant's name, postal address, IBAN, vehicle registration, the accident
  description and photographs. Photographs may show other people and number plates.
- Records are kept for the statutory ten years after the claim is closed.
- Data stays in Azure West Europe; no transfer outside the EEA.
- Customers are told in the claims flow that an automated estimate may settle their claim and
  that they can ask for a handler to review it.
